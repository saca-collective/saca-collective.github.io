# SACA - System Architects for Climate Action

## Introduction

SACA is an open collective of system architects, applying their practice to address the climate and ecological crises. We do this by collectively researching problems, designing solutions and engaging with organisations involved with climate and ecology.

Unlike traditional open-source projects that build and maintain software code, SACA focuses on the systemic level. We design vendor-agnostic reference architectures and advise organizations on how to implement these scalable blueprints. Our goal is to create an open commons of architectural patterns that accelerate global climate action.

## System Architects

System Architects are usually identified as Cloud, Solution, or Enterprise Architects within the corporate environment. SACA opens up this environment so that architects can operate "in the wild" as an open collective for the [Public Good](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_good).

This model aligns with what architects do best: analyzing complex problem spaces, designing structural system models, and governing technical standards. SACA members do not write or maintain production code; instead, they deliver the high-level blueprints that enable frontline organizations to build securely, sustainably, and without vendor lock-in.

## SACA Architectural Advisors

Contributors who participate in direct organization engagements do so under the title of **Architectural Advisors**. They act as an objective, non-commercial advisory board for public agencies, non-profits, and ecological stewards. 

## SACA Advisory Assembly

The primary vehicle for external engagement is the **SACA Advisory Assembly**. The Assembly functions as a decentralized, on-demand advisory task force comprised of our verified System Architects and Domain SMEs. 

Instead of operating as a permanent corporate board, the Assembly dynamically self-organizes around incoming organizational problems to provide objective, non-commercial technical guidance.

### The Advisory Assembly Engagement Workflow
1. **The Core Assembly**: Every contributor who actively participates in mapping the technology landscape, detailing domain gaps, or peer-reviewing patterns belongs to the global Advisory Assembly.
2. **Dynamic Assembly Call**: When an organization submits a valid technical gap, an invitation is sent out to the Assembly. A highly specialized squad of 2 to 5 matching experts (e.g., an Edge Computing Architect and a Forestry SME) assembles specifically for that organization’s intake.
3. **The Triage Session**: The assembled squad conducts a 1-to-2-hour contextual triage clinic with the organization. They evaluate local constraints, match the problem to a high-consensus SACA blueprint, and deliver an actionable technical recommendation.
4. **Dissolution & Return**: Once the localized blueprint recipe book is handed over, the specific squad dissolves, and its members return to the broader Advisory Assembly, leaving long-term software execution to the organization's delivery partners.
