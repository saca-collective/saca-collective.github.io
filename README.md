# SACA - System Architects for Climate Action

## Introduction

SACA is an open collective of system architects, applying their practice to address the climate and ecological crises. We do this by collectively researching problems, designing solutions and engaging with organisations involved with climate and ecology.

## System Architects

System Architects are usually identified as Cloud, Solution or Enterprise architects within the corperate environment. SACA opens up this environment so that architects can operate "in the wild" as an open collective for a [Public Good](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_good).

## Domains

### Ecological Conservation
*Protecting, monitoring, and restoring natural habitats, biodiversity, and wildlife.*

### Energy Transition
*Architecting the systemic shift toward distributed renewable energy and grid electrification.*

### Climate Adaptation
*Building resilient, offline-first human and technical infrastructure to withstand climate impacts.*

### Circular Economy
*Designing out waste and pollution by keeping products and materials in use.*

### Regenerative Agriculture
*Transforming food production systems to restore soil health and ecosystem vitality.*

### Climate Finance & Accounting
*Providing transparent data infrastructure to measure impact, fund projects, and prevent greenwashing.*

## Technology Landscape

### Geospatial Data Systems (GIS)
*Spatial indexing, vector/raster data pipelines, and earth observation orchestration.*

### Edge Computing & IoT Telemetry
*Low-power field networking, edge-AI inference, and offline data synchronization.*

### Digital Twins & Simulation
*Predictive scenario modeling, complex event processing, and real-time environmental replicas.*

### Big Data & Analytics
*Massive scale data lakehouses, data mesh governance, and environmental data harmonization.*

### Data Commons & Registries
*Open API design, data lineage tracking, and decentralized, interoperable asset registries.*

## Activities

### Research
Researching the domains, technology landscape and organizations.

### Analysis
*Translating raw environmental challenges and operational pain points into structured system requirements.*

### System Modeling & Design
*Creating vendor-agnostic, open-source architectural blueprints encompassing:*
- **System & Domain Models** (C4/ArchiMate structural views)
- **Data Architecture** (Schemas, ingestion models, and lineage)
- **Component Selection** (Vetting existing open-source building blocks)
- **Integration Patterns** (APIs, event-driven streaming, and messaging)
- **Infrastructure Layout** (Cloud-native, hybrid, and ruggedized edge deployment)

### Peer Review
*Operating as an open, objective Architecture Review Board (ARB) to vet community and partner designs.*

### Consultation & Advisory
*Guiding organizations away from vendor lock-in toward open, scalable technical solutions.*

## Organization Engagement

SACA architects act as a decentralized, open-source engineering and advisory task force for public or NGO climate tech projects.

### Governmental & Municipal Agencies
*Advising on smart city infrastructure, regional sensor networks, and open procurement standards.*

### Non-Profits / NGOs
*Helping grassroots and global charities design scalable, non-proprietary tech platforms.*

### Academic Institutions
*Bridging the gap between raw scientific climate models and production-ready enterprise software.*

### B-Corps & Purpose-Driven Enterprise
*Collaborating on open-source sustainability frameworks and cross-organizational data sharing.*

### Impact Investors
*Providing objective, technical due diligence to ensure green investments deliver verifiable impact.*
